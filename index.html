    How the sleeper score works

    Every skater is compared against the rest of the league on six things, then ranked from 0 to 100. A high score means several signs point the same way at once.

    Heating up
    Fantasy points per game over the last 10 games are well above his season average.
    More ice time
    His coach is playing him at least 90 seconds more per night lately. Opportunity usually comes before points.
    Power play
    Three or more power-play points in his last 10 games, a sign he's on a scoring unit.
    Shooting more
    His shots per game are up sharply. Shot volume is the most stable predictor of future goals.
    Due for goals
    He's shooting well below his career percentage. Bad puck luck tends to even out.

    Hits and blocks aren't published game by game, so last-10 numbers use his season rate for those two stats. Fantasy roster percentages aren't public, so "hide last season's top scorers" stands in for the players everyone already drafted. Stats come from the NHL's public stats feed.
